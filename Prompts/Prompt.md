The moon cast a silver glow over the quiet village as Elara wandered through its cobblestone streets. She paused at the old oak tree, where she had carved her initials years ago—a reminder of dreams once held close. A soft breeze carried whispers of laughter from the tavern nearby, where villagers shared tales beneath flickering lanterns. Elara smiled, remembering how this village had been her sanctuary through storms both literal and heartfelt. As stars bloomed above, she traced the initials again, feeling the warmth of belonging wrap around her like a familiar cloak.
